About Kimya

A student teaching students — that's the whole idea.

I started learning about AI a few months ago, and it changed how I do my own schoolwork — better projects, better research, more creative ideas. I wanted other kids to get that same head start, in a way that actually makes sense for them.

So I started running small workshops to show other students how to explore AI the fun way, while still doing the real thinking themselves.

  • 🎯 Focused on creativity, curiosity, and responsible use
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Every workshop is organized with parents, start to finish
"I don't want kids to just copy what AI says. I want them to use it to think bigger, and still know how to explain their own work."

— Kimya

How I teach

Every session follows the same simple flow, whatever the subject.

1

Explore

We start with a real question or a fun challenge — no theory dump first.

2

Create

Students build something of their own — a story, a poster, a mini project.

3

Guide

I introduce a helpful AI tool only when it fits, and always explain why.

4

Reflect & Share

Students explain their own work in their own words before we wrap up.

💡 AI is one resource I use when it helps a project — sessions are built around creativity and skill-building, not around any single app.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need any AI experience before joining?

No — the workshop is designed for complete beginners.

Who runs the workshop?

Kimya leads the teaching, with a parent/guardian handling all booking, scheduling, and payment logistics.

What will my child come away with?

One finished project they built themselves, plus a basic understanding of how to use AI as a helpful, responsible tool.

Is this a technical or coding class?

No coding required. It's a creativity and digital-skills workshop, not a programming course.
